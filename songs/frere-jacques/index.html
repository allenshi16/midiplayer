About Frère Jacques

Frère Jacques is one of the most beloved public-domain melodies available to play online. Frère Jacques is a well-known French nursery rhyme that has been sung since at least the 18th century. The song tells of a friar who overslept and is reminded to ring the morning bell. The melody is a round — it can be sung by multiple voices starting at different times, creating a harmonious canon. The tune has been adapted worldwide and is used in music education to teach rounds and canons.

As a public-domain composition, Frère Jacques can be freely performed, arranged, and studied by musicians of all levels. The melody has endured for generations because of its memorable contour and emotional resonance.

Musical structure and analysis

This arrangement is written in C major at 110 BPM. The melody is a simple four-phrase round in C major. Each phrase is four measures long, and the entire song can be sung as a perpetual canon. The first phrase establishes the theme, the second repeats it, the third introduces the "morning bell" motif, and the fourth resolves. The repetitive structure makes it ideal for teaching musical form.

Difficulty level and practice tips

Absolute beginner. The melody uses only notes from the C major scale within a narrow range. The rhythm is simple quarter notes. This is one of the first songs taught in music education worldwide.

This is the perfect piece for practicing rounds. Play the melody, then start it again from the beginning while the first iteration is still playing. Our MIDI player can help you hear how the voices interlock. Practice each phrase separately, then connect them. The song is excellent for building sight-reading confidence.

Did you know?

The melody has been adapted in cultures worldwide — in China it is used as a school morning song, in Hebrew it appears as a children's song, and in English it is one of the most common rounds taught in elementary music classes. Gustav Mahler quoted it in the third movement of his Symphony No. 1 in a minor-key, funeral-march setting.

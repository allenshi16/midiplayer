Frequently asked questions

What is public domain MIDI music?

Public-domain MIDI music reproduces melodies whose compositions are no longer (or never were) under copyright — typically works by composers who died more than 70 years ago, and traditional folk tunes. You can play, arrange and perform them without licensing.

Can I use these melodies in my own projects?

The underlying compositions are public domain, so the melodies themselves are free to use. What you hear here are our simplified single-line demo arrangements, played live in your browser rather than distributed as files.

Do you offer free MIDI file downloads?

We deliberately skip file downloads: every melody plays instantly in the built-in demo player, and the main MIDI Room player lets you load, edit, export and record your own MIDI — all locally, with nothing uploaded to a server.

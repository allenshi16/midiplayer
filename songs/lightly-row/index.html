About Lightly Row (Hänschen Klein)

Lightly Row (Hänschen Klein) is one of the most beloved public-domain melodies available to play online. Lightly Row (German: "Hänschen Klein") is a traditional German folk song that has become popular in English-speaking countries as a children's song and beginner music piece. The German lyrics tell of a little boy (Hänschen) who goes out into the world and returns home to his mother. The melody is simple and gentle, making it a popular choice for music education. The English version ("Lightly row, lightly row o'er the glassy lake") sets new lyrics to the same beloved tune.

As a public-domain composition, Lightly Row (Hänschen Klein) can be freely performed, arranged, and studied by musicians of all levels. The melody has endured for generations because of its memorable contour and emotional resonance.

The German original "Hänschen Klein" has been a staple of German elementary music education for over a century. The song's gentle melody and simple structure make it the German equivalent of "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star" as a first piece for young musicians.

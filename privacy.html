Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 2026-09-18

Your files stay local

MIDI Room parses and plays your .mid files entirely in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your MIDI files are never uploaded, stored, or shared — there is no server component that receives them.

Local storage

We use browser local storage to remember small preferences such as theme and tempo settings. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), we ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, and you can withdraw it at any time.

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.
